Method

A structured five-step process.

Each engagement follows a practical workflow designed to move from raw financial information to a forward-looking financial view.

01

Collect

Income, balances, loans, expenses, investments and recurring commitments.

02

Classify

Income, essential expenses, obligations, lifestyle spending and investments.

03

Budget

Monthly and annual budgets with expected savings and closing cash.

04

Forecast

Expected income, expenses, taxes, debt payments and planned investments.

05

Review

Compare actual results with budget and identify meaningful variances.

Opening Cash + Total Inflows − Total Outflows = Closing Cash
